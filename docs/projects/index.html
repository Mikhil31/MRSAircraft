Research programmes

Work that outlives a product cycle

Two research programmes, both running to 2032.

Planetary Research Programme

Project Vinaya

2026 to 2032

Project Vinaya is our planetary research programme, and our longest commitment. It covers planetary science and exploration, satellite and space systems, and the aircraft technologies that support them. Six years is a long time to fund a question. We think the questions worth asking take about that long.

Solar Research Programme

Mission Sunrays

2027 to 2032

Mission Sunrays is our solar research programme, built on four commitments we abbreviate as RRRS: research, reach, revolution, sustain. The work covers solar dynamics, high-precision observation, and the energy applications that follow from both. We run it with partners rather than alone.

Aircraft technologies

Both programmes feed the same place: the aircraft we build. Structures, propulsion and the materials that carry them are where planetary and solar research turn back into engineering.
