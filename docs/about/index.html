Building what India currently imports

MRS Aircraft Private Limited is an aviation engineering company registered in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

India flies more people every year than it did the year before, and buys most of the engineering behind that growth from somewhere else. We started this company to move some of that work onshore: the manufacturing, the maintenance, and the training that makes both sustainable.

Our work sits in three connected places. We build aircraft and aircraft components. We maintain the infrastructure that aircraft depend on once they are on the ground. And we train the engineers and technicians who will still be doing this in twenty years.

Mission

To bring aircraft engineering, airport maintenance and aviation training up to international standard from an Indian base, and to build the workforce that keeps it there.

Vision

An India that draws its own aircraft, maintains its own airports, and trains its own aviation workforce.

A wing is a structure first

Lift is the easy part. Everything above it is load paths: spars that carry the bending, ribs that hold the section, skin that takes the shear and closes the box.

Manufacturing, maintenance and training all read the same drawing. Build it to tolerance, inspect it on schedule, and teach the person signing it off what they are looking at.

  • Upper skin, shear panel
  • Front spar, main load path
  • Rear spar, flap and gear attachment
  • Ribs, section and stiffness
  • Stringers, skin stabilisation
  • Leading edge, formed section
  • Trailing edge flap
  • Winglet, tip device
