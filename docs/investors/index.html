Invest in us

Back a long build

Aircraft take years. Airfields take longer. If that is the horizon you invest on, here is what we are building and how to reach us.

Why this one

A real industry, not a story

Aircraft, airfields and training have customers. We are not asking you to believe a pitch deck.

Built onshore

India buys most of this engineering from abroad. That is the gap we are working in.

Measured in decades

Two research programmes committed to 2032. We are not optimising for an exit.

This is the object

Everything the money buys ends up in one place. An airframe, the engines on it, the gear it lands on, and the people who signed for every one of those before it flew.

Manufacturing builds it. Maintenance keeps it airworthy. The Academy trains the person who signs the release. The research programmes are where the next one comes from.

  • Radome and flight deck
  • Forward fuselage, frames and skin
  • Wing, torsion box and control surfaces
  • Powerplant, two high-bypass turbofans
  • Landing gear, nose and main assemblies
  • Aft fuselage and tail cone
  • Empennage, fin and stabilisers

Where the money goes

Four places, and they are the same four the rest of this site describes.

Aircraft manufacturing

Design, tooling, and the precision components that go into an airframe.

Airport maintenance

Airfields, ground support equipment, and the facilities around them.

Air Academy

Training capacity, instructors and certification.

Research programmes

Two committed to 2032, one in planetary science and one in solar.
