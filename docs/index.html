Every part has to agree

An engine is the clearest statement of what aerospace work demands. Thousands of components, each made to tolerance, each traceable, each inspected before it goes anywhere near a wing.

It is the same discipline whether the subject is an airframe, an airfield, or the person being trained to sign it off.

Fig. 01 · High-bypass turbofan, principal assemblies

  • Spinner, machined nose cone
  • Fan stage, 26 titanium blades
  • Intake lip, formed leading edge
  • Nacelle, composite cowling
  • Stator ring, 30 outlet guide vanes
  • Core, compressor and turbine
  • Exhaust nozzle and centre body
