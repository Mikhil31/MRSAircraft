Design Engineer (CAD)

Design & Engineering · Bengaluru, Karnataka · Full-time

Apply for This Role

The role

Turning engineering intent into models and drawings that a shop floor can actually build from.

What you will do

  • Produce 3D models and 2D manufacturing drawings for structural and mechanical components
  • Apply GD&T correctly and consistently
  • Support design reviews and incorporate review outcomes
  • Work with manufacturing on producibility before release, not after
  • Maintain drawing revisions and configuration records
  • Contribute to technical documentation

What you need

  • 2+ years in mechanical or aerospace design
  • Strong CAD capability: CATIA, SolidWorks, NX or equivalent
  • Sound grasp of GD&T and tolerance stack-up
  • Understanding of manufacturing processes and their constraints
  • Disciplined about revision control

Nice to have

  • Aerospace structures experience
  • FEA capability
  • Familiarity with airworthiness or certification documentation
