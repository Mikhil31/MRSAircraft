Maintenance Planner

Airport Maintenance · Bengaluru, Karnataka · Full-time

Apply for This Role

The role

You decide what gets done, when, and with what. Good planning is the difference between maintenance and firefighting.

What you will do

  • Build and maintain the preventive maintenance schedule across all asset classes
  • Manage the asset register and equipment history
  • Plan shutdowns and works windows around operational constraints
  • Coordinate spares availability with stores ahead of planned work
  • Track KPIs: completion rates, breakdown frequency, mean time to repair
  • Produce maintenance reports for management

What you need

  • 3+ years in maintenance planning or scheduling
  • Hands-on with a CMMS or maintenance management system
  • Strong spreadsheet capability and comfort with data
  • Able to read technical documentation across trades
  • Clear written communication

Nice to have

  • Airport, industrial plant or large facility background
  • Reliability-centred maintenance exposure
  • Formal planning or project management training
